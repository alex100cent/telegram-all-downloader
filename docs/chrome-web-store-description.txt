100% free and open source. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no analytics. Save Telegram media in a few clicks, directly to your device.

Your media and download history are never sent to the developer or another media service. Downloads use your existing Telegram session; settings and download history stay locally in your browser.

Save time with individual and batch downloads:

• Save photos, videos, audio, voice messages, video messages, animations, documents and stickers.
• Download individual files or select up to 200 discovered attachments and save them to one folder.
• Track progress, cancel and retry downloads in the download manager.
• Customize filenames and choose how to handle duplicate files.
• Switch between English and Russian, with light and dark themes.

Works with Telegram Web A and K, including private and protected channels you already have access to. Keep the Telegram tab open while downloading. Batches include discovered attachments, not the entire chat history.

Every feature stays free. Donations are optional, and coffee reminders can be disabled in Settings.

An independent project, not affiliated with Telegram.
